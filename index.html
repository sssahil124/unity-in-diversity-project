Food

Gujarati food balances sweet, salty and tangy in a single plate. Most dishes are vegetarian and lightly spiced, built around gram flour, jaggery, yogurt and seasonal vegetables. A full thali serves dal, kadhi, vegetables, rotli, rice and farsan (snacks) together, and sweetness in savoury dishes is the signature of the cuisine.

Wildlife

The Asiatic Lion 🦁

Gir National Park and the surrounding landscape of Saurashtra are the only wild home of the Asiatic lion. Once nearly extinct, the population has recovered through decades of conservation and community support. The census counted 674 lions in 2020 and 891 in 2025.

💡 Gir covers about 1,400 sq km, and the Maldhari herders have lived alongside the lions for generations.

🌳 ForestsDry deciduous forests of Gir
🌾 GrasslandsBanni grasslands of Kutch
🦩 WetlandsFlamingos at Nal Sarovar and the Little Rann
🌊 CoastMangroves and marine life along the Gulf of Kutch
🫏 Indian Wild AssThe Little Rann of Kutch is the last home of this rare animal, called Ghudkhur.

Gujarat is also home to blackbuck at Velavadar, the Marine National Park in the Gulf of Kutch and huge flamingo colonies in the Rann.
